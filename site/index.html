About

I started on a warehouse helpdesk in Chengdu in 2012, fixing desktops, printers and RF scanners. Over the next decade the systems got bigger: virtual desktops for a bank, then a monitoring estate of more than 30,000 hosts, then leading an on-site team and taking changes through CAB for DXC.

Most of that work was on the operations side. I saw what happens when a release lands without monitoring, or a change goes in without a rollback plan. In 2026 I started a Master's in Auckland to learn the other side properly: writing, testing and shipping software.

That combination is what I want to bring to a DevOps team in New Zealand: I can follow the code, and I know what it takes to keep it running once it ships.
